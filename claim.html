Claim CAPPY as an NFT's original minter

Each NFT recorded in the claim registry is eligible for 10,000 CAPPY. Only the wallet that first received the NFT at mint time may claim, whether it was minted through the free or paid mint. Current owners who were not the original minters cannot claim. Enter the NFT ID to check the registry.

Claim fee: 0.0003 BNB per NFT

The fee is sent to the project treasury specified in the contract. Only one NFT can be claimed per transaction. Review the transaction details in your wallet before approving.

Network: BNB Smart Chain (56). Newly minted NFTs become eligible after the project admin registers the original minter in the claim registry.
