FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total Cappybara supply?

Total supply is 10,000 NFTs, split into two phases: 5,000 free mints (tokens #1–#5,000) and 5,000 paid mints (tokens #5,001–#10,000).

Why does the free mint have a fee?

The NFT itself is free (0 BNB). You only pay a 0.0001 BNB mint fee per mint, which goes to the owner's wallet as a creator fee.

How much is the paid mint?

0.001 BNB per NFT for tokens #5,001–#10,000. The total sent is mint amount × 0.001 BNB, plus BNB Chain gas.

Can I mint without connecting a wallet manually?

Yes. Paste your wallet address in the AI Agent section — both prompts fill in automatically — then copy the prompt and send it to your AI agent with access to a BNB Chain wallet.

Can I mint with a Binance Agent ID?

Yes. If your wallet holds a Binance Agent ID, you can mint with it just like a regular wallet. After connecting, the wallet bar shows whether an Agent ID was detected, and the agent prompts above work for agent wallets too.
