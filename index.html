Prompt — Free Mint

You are an AI agent with access to my wallet on BNB Chain. Perform a FREE MINT of a Cappybara NFT for me.

Mint details:
- Collection name: Cappybara (total supply 10,000)
- Network: BNB Chain (Chain ID 56)
- Cappybara contract address: [PASTE CAPPYBARA CONTRACT ADDRESS]
- Phase: Free Mint — tokens #1 to #5,000
- Amount: 1 NFT
- NFT price: FREE (0 BNB)
- Mint fee: 0.0001 BNB per mint — this fee goes to the owner's wallet
- Total value to send: exactly 0.0001 BNB (no more)

Steps you must follow:
1. Make sure the wallet is connected to BNB Chain and the balance covers the 0.0001 BNB fee + gas.
2. Call the free-phase mint function on the Cappybara contract with a value of exactly 0.0001 BNB.
3. Wait for the transaction to confirm on BNB Chain.
4. Report back to me: the transaction hash (tx hash) and the Cappybara token ID that was minted.

Important rule: never send more than 0.0001 BNB. If the free mint phase is sold out (5,000 tokens) or the mint function is unavailable, stop and report the reason to me — do not proceed to paid mint without my permission.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the total supply of Cappybara?

Total supply is 10,000 NFTs, split into two phases: 5,000 free mint (tokens #1–#5,000) and 5,000 paid mint (tokens #5,001–#10,000).

Why does free mint have a fee?

The NFT itself is free (0 BNB). You only pay a 0.0001 BNB mint fee per mint, which goes to the owner's wallet as a creator fee.

How much is paid mint?

0.001 BNB per NFT for tokens #5,001–#10,000. The total sent is mint amount × 0.001 BNB, plus BNB Chain gas.

Can I mint without connecting a wallet manually?

Yes. Copy an AI agent prompt above — there's one for free mint and one for paid mint — and send it to your AI agent with access to a BNB Chain wallet.
